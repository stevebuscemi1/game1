How to Play

Realms of Adventure is a multiplayer text-based role-playing game where each player controls a unique character.

Game Setup

1. Start by selecting how many players will join (1-5).

2. Each player creates a character by selecting a class, race, and background.

3. Once all characters are created, the adventure begins!

Gameplay

• Players take turns in a fixed order, making decisions that shape the story.

• On your turn, you'll be presented with 3 choices tailored to your character.

• Your choices affect the narrative, your character's development, and the world around you.

• You can also enter custom commands in the command input field to interact directly with the Game Master.

Character Progression

• Characters gain XP by completing quests, defeating enemies, and solving puzzles.

• Level up at 200, 500, 1000, 2000, and 4000 XP.

• Each level increases max health, improves attributes, and unlocks new abilities.

Vision and Luck

• Vision affects what you can perceive in the game world.

• Luck influences random outcomes and critical successes/failures.

• Both can change based on your choices, items, and story events.

Saving and Loading

• Use the Save Game button to generate a save code.

• Copy and store this code to continue your adventure later.

• Use the Load Saved Game option on the start screen to resume.
